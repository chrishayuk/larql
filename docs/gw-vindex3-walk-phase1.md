# VINDEX3 semantic WALK — phase one

**Status:** GW-0 sealed; GW-0B complete; GW-3A-F retired feature postings; GW-4B closed direct single-site retrieval; GW-SUP-1 full gate not met; GW-CONV-1 nominates L24 attention; GW-HEAD-1 identifies causal L24H1; GW-KEY-1 supports a compact subject-V candidate read path; GW-READ-1 finds cheap Q/K but rejects economical composition; GW-V2 finds no registered early low-rank linear V frontier; GW-STATE-1 dependency-removal design drafted; GW-TS-1 protocol frozen before population capture, 2026-09-21
**Scope:** GW-0 + GW-1 + GW-3A-F + GW-4A/B + GW-SUP-1 + GW-CONV-1 + GW-HEAD-1 + GW-KEY-1 + GW-READ-1 + GW-V2 + GW-STATE-1 + GW-TS-1
**Claim boundary:** observational transition candidates are not causal evidence

This phase asks for the smallest cheap key that identifies the semantic edge
revealed by model execution. It does not optimise the existing layer-by-layer
gate scan and does not evaluate sparse `WalkFfn` inference.

The failed execution-support postings rung motivated the separately frozen
[GW-SUP-1 candidate-basin experiment](gw-sup-1.md). It found strong
control-separated prompt convergence and entropy concentration, but failed the
early raw-coexistence and emergence-localization conditions; the full
observational transition-superposition claim therefore did not pass. This does
not redefine GW-1 or GW-3A, and decoded basins remain non-causal evidence.

The follow-on [GW-CONV-1 operator-localization experiment](gw-conv-1.md)
removed the coexistence premise and compared every before/after write directly.
Its train-only global and four relation-specific selections all nominate L24
attention, and its preregistered held-out adjusted-gain conjunction passes.
Same-fact semantic readouts converge there, while full carrier vectors are
preserved relative to controls rather than absolutely converging. This
prioritizes norm-aware HEAD decomposition at the nominated site; it does not
identify a head or establish causality.

The resulting [GW-HEAD-1 causal attribution](gw-head-1.md) exhaustively searched
all 256 L24 head subsets on train and froze the single-head global subset H1.
Contribution-norm-matched replacement/restoration establishes H1 as necessary
and sufficient for the adjusted raw and z-scored semantic-alignment effect on
validation and test; the effect also transports to terminal logits. This opens
GW-KEY-1 source causality with immutable head identity. It does not identify a
source role, compact key, correctness mechanism, or cheap read primitive.

[GW-KEY-1](gw-key-1.md) searched six outcome-blind, template-derived source
roles through natural-Q L24H1. The frozen joint subset retains natural
`subject_entity` V alone while replacing every K with a train-derived
template/role reference. It is necessary and sufficient for the raw and
z-scored alignment effects on validation and test, including terminal-logit
transport, and therefore earns **candidate read path**. K-only selection
refused because its z-scored train sufficiency denominator was negative, so the
result identifies a compact causal payload role but not a compact natural K
subset. Natural-query construction, the structured reference-routing scaffold,
and computational efficiency remain reserved for GW-READ-1.
The passing gate is global and relation-stratified: capital, currency, and
language carry the descriptive effect, while hypernym is weak and has
non-positive test sufficiency denominators. GW-KEY-1 therefore does not support
a shared four-relation source mechanism.

[GW-READ-1](gw-read-1-results.md) prices that causal interface. A cached
template×relation Q retains essentially 100% of the held-out effect, and a
global role×ordinal K cache passes both held-out splits. The frozen L0 rank-32
V predictor does not: proximal retention falls to 0.690/0.563 raw/z on
validation and 0.782/0.785 on test. Their unchanged composition also fails
quality. Moreover, causal replay still consumes the natural carrier-before
state and seven non-H1 heads, so full pre-L24 execution remains a charged hidden
dependency. GW-READ-1 therefore localizes the remaining systems problem to
payload construction and carrier-context replacement; it does not establish
efficient WALK.

[GW-V2](gw-v2-results.md) closes the obvious payload-construction follow-up.
Its preregistered 35-cell source-depth by predictor-rank surface retained the
natural carrier and seven non-H1 heads while replacing subject/entity V. All
666 executions passed the natural/no-op and full-exact/identity controls, but
no primary cell reached the `0.80` raw or z-scored point-retention threshold on
either held-out split. There is no registered early frontier and no selected
predictor. This rules out the frozen low-rank linear family; it does not rule
out every small representation.

## The graph was easy to see. Walking it was harder.

The causal sequence now has a sharp boundary. GW-HEAD-1 isolated a small
interface that carries a measured effect, and GW-KEY-1 localized its retained
natural payload to subject/entity V. GW-READ-1 and GW-V2 did not show that this
interface can be constructed and executed without paying for the surrounding
model. In particular:

> We have isolated a small interface that carries a measured causal effect. We
> have not shown that we can construct and execute it without paying for the
> surrounding model.

A preregistered search across 35 depth/rank constructions found no early
low-rank linear reconstruction that met the held-out fidelity threshold. The
next causal rung is therefore GW-STATE-1 (design not yet in the tree): hold exact H1 fixed
and remove the entering carrier and seven non-H1 heads, alone and in
combination, to measure the smallest surrounding context that preserves the
effect. Its current document is a design draft, not a frozen protocol.

On the separate descriptive transition-support branch, earlier negative
discrete-basin lookup/conditioning results close generic basin discovery as the
automatic successor to a failed single-site key. That branch's open object is
[operator-aware multi-site transition support](gw-transition-support-paths.md):
the ordered attention and FFN contributions that physically realize a
transition.

Its [programme dependency freeze](gw-ts-1-dependencies.md) keeps the
observational and causal branches independent. ATTR-1D opens both branches. Run
subjects lead directly to population capture and GW-TS-1 prediction, while
INTERVENE-1 leads separately to ATTR-1C on the same frozen coordinates. ATTR-1C
is not a prerequisite for GW-TS-1 and its outcomes are forbidden path-selection
inputs. An executability rung opens only after independent positive verdicts from
both branches.

The shared coordinate and descriptive measurement law is frozen separately in
[ATTR-1D](v3-attr-1d-descriptive-support.md). In particular, persisted attention
weights are not treated as source contribution: source-level records require the
lossless same-run HEAD evidence needed to reconstruct each projected source
write.

## Frozen calibration input

The first calibration input is sealed before any model execution at
[bench/gw0/gemma3-4b-it-phase1/manifest.json](../bench/gw0/gemma3-4b-it-phase1/manifest.json).
It binds the exact Gemma 3 4B VINDEX3 index, system graph and tokenizer; the
source corpora; the raw completion prompts and exact BOS-bearing token IDs;
semantic triples; target tokens;
splits; and matched-control IDs. Its state is `frozen_input_unexecuted`.

The corpus contains 142 positive semantic edges and 426 executions:

| Relation | Semantic edges | Prompt families | Executions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| capital | 38 | 3 | 114 |
| currency | 34 | 3 | 102 |
| language | 30 | 3 | 90 |
| hypernym | 40 | 3 | 120 |

All prompt families for a semantic triple remain on the same relation-stratified
60/20/20 split. Each execution names a same-fact/different-prompt control and a
same-relation/different-subject control from the same split. These controls reuse
rows already in the manifest and add no executions. Multi-token target strings
retain their complete continuation token sequence, while `target_token_ids`
contains the first-token target used for next-token rank and postings recall.

The input can be rebuilt and checked without loading model weights:

```bash
python3 scripts/gw_input_manifest.py build \
  --output-dir bench/gw0/gemma3-4b-it-phase1

python3 scripts/gw_input_manifest.py validate \
  --manifest bench/gw0/gemma3-4b-it-phase1/manifest.json
```

GW-0 capture must consume this file without changing it. Its census and artifact
bundle are separate outputs sealed by `gw_phase1.py` after capture.

## First GW-0 execution

The Gemma 3 4B calibration census completed on 2026-09-20. All 426 rows passed
the observed/noop bit-parity witness and emitted all 68 declared final-position
carrier writes. The 948 MiB local bundle is at
`output/gw0-gemma3-4b-it-phase1/` and is sealed as:

```text
sha256:ac9871abdaef1206298788fae15df9121542fb1f64496cccc07bc12b5751b6bf
```

Every row contains content-addressed before/after/delta carrier arrays, a hashed
execution record, target ranks and log probabilities at each site, a transition
candidate, and top-20 exact dense gate rankings for every layer. All causal
statuses remain `untested`.

The adjudication keeps three limitations explicit:

- Gemma 3's post-attention norm is outside the current head-content adapter's
  admitted semantics, so operator/head attribution is recorded as refused for
  every row.
- The frozen input did not name a relation decoder, so relation-readable depth
  is unavailable.
- Exact WALK names feature addresses while the captured transition candidates
  name execution sites. No promoted-target annotation or execution feature
  address supplies a common edge key. The semantic/exact-WALK/transition
  disagreement matrix is therefore not identifiable from this bundle.

GW-1 and GW-3A remain locked. Treating mere presence of both result types as
agreement would erase the question this phase was designed to answer.

The descriptive stability report is bound to the sealed bundle. Its main raw
signals are relation-specific emergence (capital and language around layers
23–24, later diffuse currency, early diffuse hypernym), median zero prompt-family
emergence-layer difference with a long tail, and only modest separation between
within-relation and across-relation site/feature overlap. These are observations,
not progression gates.

## GW-0B — edge-address reconciliation

GW-0B derived the missing join from the sealed GW-0 records and the exact
container; it did not rerun any of the 426 prompts. Its final report is
`output/gw0b-gemma3-4b-it-phase1/reconciliation-report-final.json`, SHA-256:

```text
10c7af77077914c37b569d6a1c29ab06a8bec676c1e9686fe406c70873e956bc
```

The bridge has three bounded surfaces:

1. **B1 — feature promotion.** Batch `embedding · feature_down` scoring
   annotated 38,468 unique addresses: every exact-WALK address plus every
   top-100 execution-contribution address. The top-eight promoted-token rule
   is the existing `KnowledgeView` contract, not a fitted decoder. Among a
   search of up to 680 exact-WALK features with eight promoted tokens per
   feature, 81/142 unique edges had at least one promoted semantic target
   recovered somewhere in the candidate set: capital 28/38, currency 10/34,
   hypernym 14/40 and language 29/30. This is an uncalibrated recovery count,
   not semantic recall. The corresponding 426-row count is 243 hits;
   prompt-family copies are reported but are not treated as independent
   semantic edges.
2. **B2 — FFN contribution attribution.** Of 426 transition-candidate rows,
   231 contain an FFN candidate and 195 are attention-only and remain
   ineligible. Every eligible write reconstructs as
   `Σ activation_f × effective_down_f`, after the declared post-FFN norm and
   residual scale. All 231 pass the frozen scale-aware gates
   `relative_L2 <= 1e-4` and `relative_Linf <= 1e-4`; observed maxima are
   `1.774e-6` and `6.397e-6`. Attribution ranks the top 100 features by their
   post-write contribution L2 norm.
3. **B3 — disagreement matrix.** The three-way denominator is the 231 eligible
   rows only. Attention-only rows are exclusions, never execution misses.

| Semantic↔WALK | Semantic↔execution | WALK↔execution | Rows |
|---|---|---|---:|
| hit | hit | overlap | 8 |
| hit | miss | overlap | 86 |
| hit | miss | disjoint | 24 |
| miss | hit | overlap | 13 |
| miss | miss | overlap | 61 |
| miss | miss | disjoint | 39 |

The physical join exists but is sparse. Across eligible sites, exact WALK's
top 20 contains a mean 2.89% of execution's top 100 contributing addresses;
equivalently, a mean 14.44% of exact-WALK addresses appear in execution's top
100. Median site Jaccard is 0.0256. At least one address overlaps in 168/231
eligible rows, so the surfaces are neither disjoint nor close substitutes.

The first dry reconstruction used the container's source BF16 matrices and
correctly refused at about 0.52% relative error. Production execution had
selected a Q8 resident image for the large FFN projections. GW-0B therefore
materialises contribution directions from `PreparedOperands`, the effective
production-selected image; this closes the reconstruction at roughly `1e-7`
relative L2 without changing the model execution. This distinction is
load-bearing: a source-weight attribution is not an attribution of the
observed execution when lowering changed the physical weights.

These remain observational feature contributions, not causal edges. The
semantic-execution column is additionally truncated twice (execution top 100,
promotion top eight), so a miss means only "not recovered at these declared
operating points." GW-1 and the original all-row GW-3A remain locked: GW-0B
supplies a legitimate common address space for the FFN-eligible subset, while
the 195 attention-only rows still require the separate HEAD decomposition.

## GW-3A-F — execution-support postings

GW-0B earns one narrower postings experiment over its 231 reconstructed FFN
rows. GW-3A-F asks whether the existing gate-derived source-token postings are
a cheap physical addressing index for observed FFN execution support. It does
not ask whether they reproduce exact WALK, and it does not admit the 195
attention-only rows.

The protocol is frozen before the run:

- source widths are exactly `{1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,256}` features per source
  token per layer; no interpolation or selected operating point is allowed;
- execution targets are the independently frozen top `{20,50,100}` GW-0B
  contribution rankings;
- address recall and contribution-L2-mass recall are both primary report
  surfaces and neither may substitute for the other;
- the progression gate is top-100 micro address recall `>= 95%` at
  address-weighted eligible-universe coverage `<= 10%` on the unmasked
  postings curve;
- candidate fraction always divides by the lookup's eligible address universe:
  all declared dense FFN layers for the unmasked curve, and the single observed
  execution layer only for the explicitly non-progressive oracle-layer curve;
- controls are exact dense WALK with candidate counts matched independently in
  each layer, a deterministic layer/count-matched random control, and the
  non-progressive oracle execution-layer restriction;
- semantic target recovery is only a lower bound where a postings address was
  already annotated by GW-0B. Unannotated candidates are not semantic misses;
  supported-causal recall remains `N/A` until an intervention supplies it;
- the earlier 81/142 observation is phrased only as target occurrence somewhere
  in the searched WALK set. Its calibration control holds every WALK address
  and layer fixed while replacing targets with non-target vocabulary IDs of the
  exact same frequency in the sealed prompts plus target continuations;
- the eight semantic/WALK/execution agreements receive a descriptive audit
  only, including relation, emergence, target confidence, prompt family,
  contribution concentration, WALK entropy, largest-contributor dominance and
  the number of distinct source-token postings that independently hit support.

Interpretation is declared in advance: high address and mass recall means a
stable support set; low address but high mass recall means dominant computation
without stable support identity; high address but low mass recall misses the
important computation; low values on both surfaces fail postings as execution
support.

## Independent evidence surfaces

Every GW-0 row keeps these surfaces separate:

| Surface | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `semantic_edge` | External known relation, or a separately marked negative/discovery |
| `exact_walk_result` | Dense VINDEX3 `KnowledgeView` gate scan |
| `transition_candidate` | Structure inferred from OBS/LENS/HEAD execution |
| `causal_status` | `untested`, `supported`, or `refuted`; evidence IDs are mandatory for the latter two |

Before GW-5, the primary execution metric is **transition-candidate
retention/recall**. **Supported-causal recall** is reported only over rows already
carrying sealed intervention evidence. No observation or attribution can set that
status by itself.

The sealed census labels positive semantic edges as the `primary` cohort. Semantic
negatives and discoveries use separate `negative` and `discovery` cohorts and do not
enter the main retrieval denominator. The validator refuses a semantic status placed
in the wrong cohort.

## GW-0 — immutable execution-edge census

Initial scale is four relations, 40–60 edge families per relation and three prompt
semantic families, including at least one non-factual/WordNet-style relation. The
split unit is:

```text
(subject canonical identity,
 relation,
 target canonical identity,
 prompt semantic family)
```

All aliases, chat-template variants, paraphrases and trivial lexical rewrites of an
edge family remain on the same split.

Each `larql.gw0.census-row.v1` row contains:

```text
edge_id, edge_family_id, split, cohort
semantic_edge { status, subject, relation, target,
                target_token_ids[], prompt_semantic_family }
exact_walk_result { hits[{layer, feature, rank?, score?}] }
transition_candidate? {
    sites[{layer, site, operator_ids?}],
    feature_addresses[{layer, feature}]?
}
causal_status
causal_evidence_ids[]
provenance {
    model_identity, container_identity, execution_fingerprint,
    basis_identity, run_record_hash, position
}
artifacts[{kind?, path, sha256, bytes?, dtype?, shape?}]
```

Large carrier states, writes and head records remain external. The `seal` command in
[gw_phase1.py](../scripts/gw_phase1.py) verifies their SHA-256 digests, rejects split
leakage and causal relabelling, and writes a bundle manifest binding the census,
artifacts and plan-derived site universe. Every derived result names that bundle
hash. The census is never amended with analysis output.

The main GW-0 report is the disagreement matrix among semantic truth, exact WALK and
transition candidates, plus within-relation versus across-relation stability for
read depth, site/operator identity, write direction and exact-WALK feature identity.

## GW-1 — relation-only structural pruning

Input is only `semantic_edge.relation`. Training counts transition-candidate sites
on the training split and freezes a stable site ranking per relation. Evaluation is
on held-out edge families. The complete curve is reported:

```text
transition-candidate retention
    versus
eligible sites / all plan sites
```

The primary progression region is at least 95% retention. A relation that requires
most sites has failed structural pruning; its result is not rescued by choosing a
post-hoc threshold. Relations absent from training retain zero candidates rather
than receiving subject-derived fallback information.

## GW-3A — inverted postings baseline

`KnowledgeView::build_posting_index` builds the non-learned baseline directly from
the executable VINDEX3 semantic roles. Its source-key vocabulary is frozen before
evaluation and may contain held-out subject tokens, but construction consumes no
edge labels:

```text
declared token --abs(feature_gate dot embedding)--> top feature addresses
feature address --embedding dot feature_down--------> promoted token
```

A query unions the subject token postings and optionally intersects them with the
frozen GW-1 layer mask. No gate dot products run at lookup time. Features per source
token and targets per feature are operating points, never fitted parameters. The
declared key vocabulary avoids an unnecessary full-vocabulary-by-feature build and
makes the baseline's admitted query domain explicit.

Every lookup is written separately as `larql.gw3a.lookup.v1`:

```json
{
  "schema": "larql.gw3a.lookup.v1",
  "edge_id": "...",
  "operating_point": "source-k=8/unmasked",
  "candidate_features": [{"layer": 23, "feature": 91}],
  "promoted_token_ids": [1234],
  "costs": {
    "candidate_features": 1,
    "total_features": 348160,
    "gate_bytes": 0,
    "other_bytes": 24,
    "dot_products": 0,
    "index_bytes": 12000000,
    "wall_time_ns": null
  }
}
```

Reports keep semantic-target recall, exact-WALK feature recall,
transition-candidate address recall and supported-causal address recall separate.
They additionally report edge retention (at least one address recovered) so a row
with several observational candidates cannot silently change the denominator.
Candidate fraction, gate bytes, other bytes, dot products, index bytes and wall time
are never collapsed into one cost score.
The desired region is at least 95% transition-candidate recall while examining at
most 5–10% of features and touching at most 10% of exact-scan bytes. Curves decide;
an isolated threshold does not.

### GW-3A-F — FFN execution-support rung

GW-0B permits a narrower experiment over its 231 reconstructed FFN sites. The
frozen contract is
[gw3af-execution-support-postings.md](gw3af-execution-support-postings.md),
with a machine-readable preregistration bound to the sealed artifacts. It
separates top-100 execution-support address recall from top-100 contribution-L2
mass recall, uses the eligible address universe for each row and layer mask as
the coverage denominator, and fixes the width ladder to
`{1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,256}`. Exact-WALK matched-candidate, matched-null and
oracle-layer arms are controls; the oracle arm is non-progressive.

The sealed Gemma 3 4B result is negative: width 256 recovers 9.79% of the
top-100 support addresses and 16.63% of their recorded contribution-L2 mass at
3.21% coverage. Candidate-count-matched exact WALK is effectively identical;
layer-matched random reaches 3.23% address recall. This establishes some gate
enrichment above chance but rejects stable gate-posting addressability for the
FFN-eligible subset. It justifies operator/execution-derived keys for that
subset. The original all-row GW-3A decision still awaits the separate HEAD
surface for the 195 attention-only rows.

### GW-4A — transition/write families

GW-3A-F's negative result moves the retrieval object from feature addresses to
observed carrier writes. The frozen GW-4A contract and result are recorded in
[gw4-transition-write-families.md](gw4-transition-write-families.md).

On the 231 reconstructed FFN writes, same-fact prompt variants repeat at the
same layer with median cosine `0.8330`, and exact-layer write similarity
separates same from different relations with AUC `0.9065` (`p = 0.000999` under
the block-stratified permutation). A training-write family index reaches
`0.7777` balanced relation accuracy on 88 held-out writes versus `0.5579` for
the exact-layer prior, with refusals counted as misses. Relation-specific SVD
subspaces at ranks 2, 4, 8 and 16 also pass the frozen family gate.

This is observational evidence for reusable write families, not yet an access
method. The preregistered rule opens GW-4B to test whether carrier-before plus
relation/context and layer/site can retrieve those families within explicit
candidate, byte and compute budgets. All 231 causal statuses remain `untested`.

GW-4B finds strong but non-progressive pre-write enrichment. At `K=1`, raw and
low-rank carrier keys cover `81.82%` of the 88 assessment writes while examining
a mean `18.96%` of the eligible same-relation/layer exemplars. Median retrieved
delta cosine is `0.86–0.89` and median oracle gap is zero. The best frozen
relation-conditioned arm nevertheless reaches only `0.0919` at the fifth
percentile of the fact-block bootstrap advantage, below its `0.10` gate. No
operating point opens GW-4C.

The state-only ablation clears the numeric thresholds (`0.1784` bootstrap p05)
but the frozen evaluator excludes ablation arms from progression. It motivates
confirmation on new edge families; it does not retroactively change this
decision. The primary path therefore moves above a single write to an ordered,
operator-aware support trajectory. It does not return to static basin labels.

## Decision after phase one

The decision record answers only:

1. Is there stable structural locality? — GW-1.
2. Is there stable addressable feature identity? — GW-3A.
3. Where does exact WALK disagree with observed execution? — GW-0.

GW-3A-F rejects gate-derived feature identity as the FFN execution-support
address. GW-4A establishes reusable observational write families on the same
sealed subset. GW-4B does not clear its frozen uncertainty gate, so GW-4C remains
closed. GW-SUP-1 and prior discrete-basin work also reject generic basin lookup
as the untested fallback. GW-CONV-1 supplies a concrete L24 attention event at
which to begin decomposing that path. The primary object is now an ordered, operator-typed
`TransitionSupportPath`, with HEAD-OBS-1 attention support and GW-0B-style FFN
support beneath one transition identity. The state-only carrier result remains
a preregistered replication candidate, not a promoted access-path claim.
