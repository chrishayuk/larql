# Native writable records: campaign synthesis

Consolidated 2026-09-05. **Durable result: reversible native value control with
a measured addressing failure. Selective writable entity–relation records have
not been established.** The bounded twelve-slot gate/up-fitting branch is now
closed after its one controlled optimization correction failed development.

This campaign runs in the isolated `exp/native-record-composition` worktree.
It is a research instrument, not a shipped LQL feature. No checkpoint, base
vindex or LARQL runtime crate was modified by this campaign. Prior protocols,
scripts, scored artifacts and failed attempts are retained.

## Scope and claim being tested

Native experiments use the same original **Gemma 3 4B IT**, unquantized bf16
checkpoint, L26, and single-token values. The recovered editor writes a
detector into gate/up rows and an embedding-derived value direction into down
columns. The three-record demonstration was expanded to four entities × three
relations, with distinct values within each relation.

The desired result was not merely a changed factual completion. It was a write
whose entity–relation binding could be addressed across wording, replaced
without changing neighboring bindings, rejected on uninstalled queries, and
eventually consumed as an intermediate in another operation. All reported
answer accuracies below use the full vocabulary at the next-token position;
they do not establish free-form generation or internal multi-hop composition.

## What the evidence supports

1. **A reliable intervention platform.** Installation, down-column replacement,
   removal and restoration are reproducible; logged full-model restoration
   checks recover reference logits exactly. Actual MLP inputs, gate/up scores,
   activation sequences, normalization effects and answer margins are captured.
2. **Value control through native computation.** Selected installed slots can
   cause distinct values to win against the full vocabulary through the real
   remaining model. Wrong-record and value-only counter-swaps distinguish this
   from generic output forcing, while also exposing leakage.
3. **A concrete failed controller target.** Canonical binding separation is
   feasible, and C+A demonstrates useful activation patterns. The tested native
   rows have not learned to produce those patterns with dependable cross-form
   selection and rejection. Completing training delivery does not close that gap.

## What the evidence does not support

- Selective writable records, reliable rejection of uninstalled bindings, or
  database-like isolation. Positive-answer gains cannot outweigh the direct
  negative-control and value-swap failures for that claim.
- A capacity bound for a single native slot, or necessity/sufficiency of larger
  feature bundles. The gate-loss correction proves the previous training misses
  were not an unavoidable consequence of the twelve-slot architecture on those
  training examples; it says nothing exhaustive about all fitting procedures.
- Native composition, a causal interpretation of the pretrained feature graph,
  or sparse-execution acceleration. Those are distinct research questions and
  were not advanced by this campaign.
- A universal value multiplier. Euro reaches 7/7 at 4× under the earlier oracle,
  but only 1/7 under actual routing; saturation, margins and collateral prevent
  treating that strength diagnostic as a general prescription.

## Rejection and data qualifications

The cached follow-up separates score separability from native quietness. With
training-selected peak thresholds, the prior fits retain 7/24 and 10/24 correct
validation positives; the first still accepts one of four near names. Even
optimistic validation-only threshold choices satisfying every negative-category
gate retain only 3/24 and 14/24. This is poor separation on the first roster and
partial coverage on the second, not evidence that reject-all is useful or that
all conceivable readers fail. Diagnostic thresholds were never deployed.

The follow-up also identified an inherited split weakness: modulo-three
negative partitioning puts the near-name and uninstalled-entity **capital**
negatives in validation, with currency/language negatives in training. The split
was kept fixed for the controlled correction and its relation counts reported.
It limits interpretations of the development gap; it does not undo the separate
full-model evaluation leakage. A future data redesign would be a new protocol,
not a retroactive reclassification of this run as a success.

The development and evaluation banks become observed diagnostic data after
their use. They must not be described as untouched in any follow-up. Aliases
contain explicit identity declarations. Negative retention measures agreement
with clean outputs, not necessarily objective truth; a changed answer can be
factually reasonable and still violate locality.

## Keep the recovered 12B composition result separate

The recovered [FHG-2 artifact](RESULTS.md#recovered-fhg-2-result) is a different
12B setup, not this 4B native-write experiment. Correct and corrupt **textual**
intermediates select their corresponding paths in 16/16 cases each. But with
the first edge absent and no supplied intermediate, the baseline still chooses
C in 11/16 cases. Textual intermediate control is strong; necessity of the
first binding remains unresolved. No native-composition run was added here.

## Disposition

**Closed:** the present bounded gate/up-fitting branch, after the cached-only
auxiliary correction fails the frozen development prerequisite. No full-model
evaluation of that correction is warranted by the results.

**Retained:** the reversible editing instrument, all fixtures and contrasts,
the useful C+A intervention target, and measured evidence of addressing failure.

**Deferred:** native composition, capacity scaling, feature bundles, graph
causality and HNSW/sparse execution. A richer controller is a new hypothesis
requiring its own justification, data boundary and success gate, not the next
automatic rung of this campaign.
